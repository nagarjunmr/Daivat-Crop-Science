Micronutrient Mixture Fertilizer

Karnataka Notified Grade I (1)

For Black Soil With Alkaline Reaction

Composition

Karnataka Grade I (1) — Zn–Fe–Mn–B

MicronutrientDeclared value*
Zinc (Zn)3
Iron (Fe)2
Manganese (Mn)1
Boron (B)0.51

*The label lists 3–2–1–0.51 in the order Zn–Fe–Mn–B; units are not specified in the supplied image.

Benefits Listed on the Label

  • Readily available and easily assimilated nutrition that contributes to respiratory functions within plants.
  • Amino acids and minerals described as precursors for phytohormones and other growth substances, supporting metabolic and physiological activities.
  • Support for energy metabolism, pollination, fruit formation, chlorophyll concentration and photosynthesis.

Crop & Application

Crops: All crops

Foliar Application — Dosage Panel

Dose: 2.0–2.5 ml/l of water

Application: 3–6 applications at 20–30 days after transplanting, as printed on the label.

Drip Application

Dose: 1.0–2.0 l/acre

Application: 2–4 applications every 7–10 days from early in the crop cycle.

Standard Dose of Application — Separate Label Panel

Method: Foliar application

Dose: 0.5–1.0 ml per litre of water

The label states that the application dose increases depending upon the severity of zinc deficiency.

Dosage clarification: The supplied label gives two different foliar doses: 0.5–1.0 ml/l and 2.0–2.5 ml/l. Confirm the correct dose and foliar application schedule with the manufacturer before use.
